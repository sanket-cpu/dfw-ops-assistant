# DFW Airport – HVAC Context for Maintenance Technicians

---

## 1. Terminal Layout Overview

Dallas Fort Worth International Airport (DFW) consists of 5 main passenger terminals:
- **Terminal A**: Domestic flights, 26 gates, built 1974, renovated 2017.
- **Terminal B**: Domestic flights, 47 gates, American Eagle regional hub.
- **Terminal C**: Domestic flights, 28 gates, features frequent crew/ops HVAC loads.
- **Terminal D**: International terminal, 26 gates, highest passenger volume, 80-foot ceilings.
- **Terminal E**: Mixed domestic/international, 41 gates, several multi-story open areas.

Each terminal is climate-controlled by distributed HVAC units along concourses, gate houses, and service areas.

---

## 2. Dallas-Fort Worth Climate Parameters

- **Climate Zone:** Hot-humid (ASHRAE Zone 2A)
- **Summer highs:** 100–118°F (38–48°C)
- **Winter lows:** 25–45°F (−4–7°C)
- **Humidity:** Average summer relative humidity: 60–80%
- **Severe weather events:** Thunderstorms, high winds (up to 150 mph for design load), occasional ice storms.

Special HVAC requirements:
- Maintain 72–76°F (22–25°C) for passenger areas year-round
- Dehumidification required in all terminals
- Fast recovery after external door openings

## 4. Airport-Specific Requirements

**Continuous, 24/7 operation:** All passenger service climate systems must be online at all times; most units have alarms for temperature drift > ±2°F.
- **Downtime impact:** Affects passenger comfort, staffing productivity, and regulatory compliance; critical for international customs areas.

**Passenger Comfort Priority:** Maintain temp 72–76°F and 40–55% RH; extra cooling during summer boarding.
- Terminal D and E: Special rapidly recoverable air curtains at main doors.

**Noise Restrictions:** Maximum allowable noise levels: <65 dBA at passenger gates. All rooftop units must be fitted with upgraded sound baffles.

**Energy Efficiency Mandates:** All HVAC retrofits must conform to ASHRAE 90.1-2016 and include variable frequency drives (VFDs) where feasible.

**Security Zoning:** Maintenance staff must badge into equipment rooms. HVAC zones map 1:1 to security sectors for access control.

---

## 5. Building Automation System (BAS) Protocols

- **All main HVAC systems are integrated with BAS (Johnson Controls Metasys v8.0).**
- **System Features:**
    - Remote monitoring and alarm notification (temperature, humidity, filter DP, fault codes)
    - Schedule override capabilities for special events
    - Fault escalation tree: Technician -> Supervisor -> Facilities Manager
- **Access Control:** Only licensed facility techs can change BAS settings; all overrides logged.
- **Common BAS Alarms:**
    - AHU Filter Differential Pressure >2.0" w.g.
    - Chiller Compressor Fault (Code C42)
    - Supply Air Temp Out of Range (>80°F or <55°F)
    - High Condensate Level
    - Door Held Open >2 min (for energy loss tracking)
